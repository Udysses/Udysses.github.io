EYP Case Bot

A private Discord assistant for case administration.

The EYP Case Bot is a Discord application used within a single private community to open, track and document internal cases. It organises case records and generates the associated documents in the operator's own Google Drive.

It is not a public service and is not available for general sign-up. For how it handles data, see the privacy policy.
