Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information the EYP Case Bot ("the bot") processes and how it is used. The bot is a private Discord application operated for a single community and is not offered to the public.

Information the bot processes

  • Discord identifiers and display names of members who use its commands, so it can attribute case actions and check permissions.
  • Case records entered through the bot, such as case titles, notes, status and the members assigned to a case.
  • Documents the bot generates on the operator's behalf, which are stored as files in the operator's own Google Drive.

How Google user data is used

The bot requests a single, limited Google Drive permission (drive.file). This grants access only to the files and folders the bot itself creates or is explicitly given, and never to the rest of the Google account's Drive. The bot uses this permission solely to create and organise case documents for the operator. It does not read, collect or transmit any other files in the account.

How information is shared

Information is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. It is used only to provide the bot's features within the community it serves. Documents remain in the operator's own Google Drive under their control.

Data retention

Case records are kept for as long as the community needs them for its internal administration, and may be removed on request to the operator. Generated documents remain in the operator's Google Drive until the operator deletes them.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about data the bot holds can be sent to hellenicrepublicrobloxholder@gmail.com.

EYP Case Bot is a private, non-commercial Discord application.
